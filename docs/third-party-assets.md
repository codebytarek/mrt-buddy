# Third-party assets

## NFC reading animation

- Source: [NFC Reading on LottieFiles](https://lottiefiles.com/free-animation/nfc-reading-IgMDajnDf2)
- Creator: invictus
- Original bundled asset: `composeApp/src/commonMain/composeResources/files/nfc-reading.json`
- Theme-ready animation: `composeApp/src/commonMain/composeResources/files/nfc-reading-theme.json` (the center/background fills are removed and animation strokes are recolored at runtime to match the screen).
- License: the source page lists it as free to use under the [Lottie Simple License](https://lottiefiles.com/page/license). Attribution is not required; attribution is included here as a courtesy.
- The app recolors the monochrome animation mark to the active Material theme text color in memory; the original asset remains available unchanged.
